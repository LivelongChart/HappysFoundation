02

Veterinary Partner

Happy's is building a network of veterinary partners committed to improving animal welfare across Harris County. Partner clinics can support emergency rescue cases, community animal care, and spay/neuter initiatives while connecting a broader network of care. Together, we're creating a more connected and sustainable approach to animal welfare in Houston.

03

Community and Events Partner

Happy's is building a network of community and event partners committed to expanding animal welfare across Harris County. Partner organizations can provide venues, sponsorships, and collaborative opportunities for fundraisers, adoption events, educational programs, and community outreach. Together, we're creating stronger connections among local businesses, neighborhoods, and the animals that need them most.
