About Happy's

Our Story

Years ago, a stray cat named Happy changed everything.

Named after the loyal blue cat from Fairy Tail, Happy became a reminder of a simple belief: when something vulnerable needs help, you show up. But Happy was taken away before that belief could be fully honored, and that loss never left.

On May 26, 2026, it happened again. A critically injured kitten was found alone in a Houston parking lot. Emergency care was rushed. Everything that could be done was done. It wasn't enough.

But in his final moments, even through the pain, he was making biscuits.

Happy's was created from that moment.

Not from a business plan. Not from a grant opportunity. From the conviction that no animal should spend its last moments, or any moment, without someone willing to show up. Every service booked, every youth employed, every TNR operation funded exists to make sure that when it matters most, we're already there.

Meet the Team

Arya's Story

Years ago, a stray cat named Happy changed everything for me.

I named him after the loyal blue cat from Fairy Tail, because Happy, the character, shows up for the people he loves no matter what. My Happy became a reminder of that same simple belief: when something vulnerable needs help, you show up. But Happy was taken away before I could fully honor that belief, and that loss never left me.

On May 26, 2026, it happened again. I found a critically injured kitten alone in a Houston parking lot. I rushed him to emergency care. I did everything I could. It wasn't enough.

But in his final moments, even through the pain, he was making biscuits.

For those who don't know, when a cat kneads, it means comfort. Safety. It means they feel held. That kitten, who had been alone and broken in a parking lot just hours before, died making biscuits because I had shown up. Because he wasn't alone anymore.

I built Happy's from that moment.

From the conviction that no animal should be alone, not in their final moments, not in any moment.

Zain's Story

I have always been an animal person. Growing up, I cared for many birds and grew a deep love and respect for the responsibility that comes with looking after animals. For the majority of my life, I was never a cat person, at least not at first.

That changed when a feral couple started showing up in our backyard. They wanted nothing to do with us, but they kept coming back for the food and water we left out. Over time, by consistently caring for the cats, I felt like I began to love them and their habits. After enough time and consideration, I decided I wanted and was prepared to finally adopt my own cat.

Then I got my first kitten, which skyrocketed my love for cats. The first night, he refused to sleep in his bed until I placed it right next to my pillow on my bed. He hasn’t slept anywhere else since that moment. He immediately became part of my soul.

I grew up watching animals in my area not get the care and protection they deserved, time and time again. Not because people didn't care, but because the resources, the urgency, and the infrastructure were never there to match the scale of the problem. Too many animals suffer alone, and for most of them, no one ever shows up.

Happy's is my answer to that. Built on the belief that every animal deserves to have someone to support and help them get the love they deserve, and that with the right model, we can make sure far more of them do. We will reach as many animals as we possibly can, and fight to give every one of them a better life
