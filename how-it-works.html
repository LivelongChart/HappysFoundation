How Happy's works

Two Problems.
One Model.

Happy's begins with the customer's need, identifies the appropriate kind of provider, and creates a fair transaction that can do more than complete the job.

  1. 01

    Tell Happy's what you need.

    A customer describes the local service or project.

  2. 02

    Happy's identifies the provider type.

    The work is assessed for an appropriate youth, adult, or qualified professional path.

  3. 03

    An eligible vetted provider accepts.

    Service eligibility depends on age, experience, safety requirements, and applicable law.

  4. 04

    The provider completes the work and gets paid.

    Provider compensation is part of the model—not an afterthought.

  5. 05

    The marketplace earns platform or service revenue.

    Operating activity creates revenue and enterprise value.

  6. 06

    Remaining economic value can support animal welfare.

    After providers are compensated and operating costs are covered, the intended model can help support the separate nonprofit mission.

The matching rule

Right job.
Right provider.
No shortcuts.

Each request is matched according to the provider’s age, experience, the work’s safety requirements, and applicable law.

Connected by purpose.
Distinct in focus.

Social-enterprise marketplace

  • Operates the local-services marketplace

  • Creates paid work opportunities
  • Generates earned revenue and enterprise value

The customer value

Fair work at a fair market price—with more value created around it.
