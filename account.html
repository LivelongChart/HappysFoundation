Marketplace safety

Personal information stays out of public profiles.

Future accounts must use private messaging, role-based visibility, verification, reporting, and controlled address sharing. Exact service addresses should be disclosed only inside an authenticated workflow when necessary.

Minor identities, home addresses, phone numbers, schedules, and precise locations must never be publicly displayed. Guardian consent and stricter access controls must be possible before youth features activate.
