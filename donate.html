Supplement the engine

Yes, we accept donations.

The animal-welfare nonprofit is designed to be less reliant on passive giving while continuing to accept donations and grants as supplemental support. Donations can supplement support created by the local-services marketplace's earned-revenue engine.

How support fits

Not the whole engine.
Still important fuel.

Earned

Youth are compensated and operating expenses are covered. Remaining service revenue supports the animal-welfare mission.

Supplemental

Donations and grants can help the mission respond, grow, and do more than earned revenue covers alone.

Patient

Happy's may also pursue interest-free impact loans as another supplemental source of support.
