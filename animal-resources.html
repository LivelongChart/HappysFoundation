Resource quality standard

Reviewed does not mean guaranteed.

Future review may consider welfare policies, transparency, current licensing or accreditation where applicable, foster and adoption practices, veterinary practices, inspection or enforcement information, credible reporting, documented legal actions, documented welfare violations, organizational responses, intake policies, and current operating status.

Happy's will not label an organization abusive based on rumor, hearsay, one anonymous review, or an unverified social-media allegation. We also will not claim that an organization has never caused harm.

“No-kill” language will appear only as a carefully sourced organizational claim, not as a blanket guarantee of quality.

Conditions can change

See a concern with a listed resource?

Future concerns will be handled through a private moderation process. Credible, substantiated welfare concerns can result in a resource being hidden or removed while it is reviewed.
