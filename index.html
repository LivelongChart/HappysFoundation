The model

Two problems.
One model.

Happy's connects vetted Houston youth with families who need services — and turns every dollar earned into emergency rescue, spay/neuter access across Harris County, and community awareness. Youth build real work experience. Animals get help that isn't waiting on policy to catch up.

Youth get hired

Vetted Houston teens take on pressure washing, lawn care, window cleaning, and tutoring — earning fair pay and real experience.

Every dollar earned

Work is the engine, not donations. Revenue from services is what keeps the mission funded and sustainable.

Animals get rescued

Funds go straight to emergency cases, safe spaces, and spay/neuter access for the animals most at risk of suffering alone.

Animal welfare

One animal at a time.

Millions of animals. One consistent solution. Houston is where we start.

Right now, the system isn't fixed. It won't be tomorrow. But every emergency case that gets funded, every animal that finds a safe space, every spay/neuter surgery that happens — that's one less animal the system failed. We're not waiting for policy to catch up. We're working in the gap it left behind.
