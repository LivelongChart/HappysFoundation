Hire our services

Book a current Happy's service.

Houston families and businesses can request work from eligible, vetted providers. Providers are compensated, operating costs are covered, and remaining economic value can help support the separate animal-welfare mission.

Current verified services are chess tutoring, academic tutoring, mobile hand car wash, and power washing.

Academic tutoring can cover school subjects that match a provider’s ability. Mobile Hand Car Wash is a basic hand-wash service that may include an exterior hand wash, hand drying, basic wheel cleaning, and a simple interior vacuum or wipe-down where appropriate. It does not include advanced detailing, ceramic coating, paint correction, mechanical work, or vehicle repair. Power washing is matched only where the provider’s age, experience, and safety requirements make it appropriate.

Service eligibility depends on age, experience, safety requirements, and applicable law.

Join the team

Paid, flexible work for eligible Houston youth.

Happy's provides paid work opportunities for applicants age 14 and older. Team members choose their availability and can be matched with local clients for appropriate current services.

Parent or guardian consent is required for minors. Applicants 18 or older may require background screening.
