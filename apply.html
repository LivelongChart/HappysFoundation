Current youth provider track / Age 14+

Choose your availability.
Build experience by doing.

Applicants select the services they can offer and the availability that works for them. Service eligibility depends on age, experience, safety requirements, and applicable law.

  1. 01

    Submit your application

    Share your age, contact information, availability, service interests, and where you can work.

  2. 02

    Introduction call

    A Happy's team member may follow up to learn who you are and what you can do.

  3. 03

    Vetting and approval

    Parent or guardian consent is required for minors. Applicants 18 or older may require basic background screening.

  4. 04

    Accept appropriate work

    Approved providers choose their availability, connect with clients, complete eligible assignments, and get paid.
