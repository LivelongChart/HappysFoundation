A submission is a request for review, not a promise of placement.

Happy's does not guarantee intake, rescue, veterinary care, foster placement, adoption, transportation, funding, or emergency response. Public listings will use only a broad neighborhood, city, or approximate region. Exact animal or foster locations, home addresses, precise coordinates, private phone numbers, personal email, minor information, and internal safety details will not be displayed.

If an animal or person is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency, veterinary, animal-control, or law-enforcement resource.
