Finding responsible help

Start with safety.Share only what should be public.

Animal submissions are reviewed before publication. Public listings should use only a broad neighborhood, city, or approximate region—never a home address, exact animal location, foster address, personal contact information, or precise map coordinates.

Urgent injury, immediate danger, or suspected active abuse requires a different, private review path and may require professional assistance rather than a standard listing.
