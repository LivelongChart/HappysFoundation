The Global Picture

Over 200 million dogs live as strays worldwide, and the global stray cat population sits between 480 and 600 million. In 72% of countries, no national policy addresses stray animal management. In the U.S. alone, 670,000 shelter animals are euthanized every year.

02

Houston Specifically

Harris County animal control receives about 30,000 calls per year, and BARC's intake reached 22,445 animals in 2025, up 28% since 2022. Houston is estimated to have 750,000 to 1.2 million stray animals. The shelter isn't the problem. It's where the problem ends up.

What Actually Works

Most people's first instinct when they see a stray is to walk away. Happy's exists to change that instinct. Prevention works. Early intervention works. And showing up works. The only thing that has to change is the default.

Where Happy's Fits

Happy's is designed to help fund the gap between finding an animal and getting it help. Earned revenue provides an additional engine alongside supplemental donations, grants, and potential interest-free impact loans. Providers are paid and operating costs are covered before remaining economic value can support the animal-welfare mission.
