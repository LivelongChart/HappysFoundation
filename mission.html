What Must Change

Four directions for lasting progress.

01

Prevent unnecessary suffering

Improve access to resources, foster support, veterinary care, and earlier intervention so fewer animals fall through the cracks.

02

Change policies and practices

Research and advocate for stronger protections, better accountability, and more humane institutional practices.

03

Change how people think

Make reliable research accessible, challenge harmful assumptions, and encourage people to reconsider how animals are treated.

04

Build change beyond Houston

Develop an effective local model, learn from results, and ultimately help improve animal welfare in other communities.
