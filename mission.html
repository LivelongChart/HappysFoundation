Our Mission / Houston

Why Happy’s exists.

To protect vulnerable lives, animal and human, by employing Houston youth in purpose-driven work that funds compassionate care for the animals most at risk of suffering alone.

The two-entity model

Animal welfare is the purpose. Earned revenue helps sustain it.

Entity one

Social-enterprise marketplace

Happy's matches customer needs with appropriate eligible providers, creates paid opportunities, and generates earned revenue and economic value.

Explore the marketplace →

Entity two

Animal-welfare nonprofit

After providers are compensated and operating expenses are covered, remaining revenue can help support the animal-welfare mission.

Explore animal welfare →

Why it is built this way

Earned revenue works alongside donations, grants, potential interest-free impact loans, and partnerships so the mission can draw from more than one source of support.
