The two-entity model.

Animal welfare is the purpose.Earned revenue helps sustain it.

Happy's is intended to operate through two connected entities. The animal-welfare nonprofit carries out and supports the mission. Alongside it, the social-enterprise marketplace matches customer needs with appropriate eligible providers, creates paid opportunities, and generates earned revenue and enterprise value.

Providers are compensated and operating expenses are covered. Remaining economic value can help support the animal-welfare mission. This model is intended to reduce reliance on passive donations and grants without rejecting them.

Donations, grants, and interest-free impact loans may provide supplemental support.
